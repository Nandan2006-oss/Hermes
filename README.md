Hermes — AI-powered product intelligence for industrial commerce

Industrial companies deal with product data scattered across websites, catalogs, and technical documents — turning that mess into clean, structured, commerce-ready listings takes hours of manual work per product.

Hermes fixes that. Give it whatever you have on a product — a name, a rough description, a spec sheet PDF, a supplier link — and it returns a complete structured product record: every attribute filled in, validated for consistency, and enriched where data is missing.

What makes it trustworthy rather than just automated: every generated field comes with its source and a confidence score. If two sources disagree, Hermes flags the conflict instead of silently picking one. If data genuinely isn't there, it says so instead of guessing. And it's built to work at catalog scale — processing dozens of products at once, not just one hero example.

The goal is simple: take a messy, incomplete product listing and turn it into something a catalog manager could trust and ship, without having to double-check the AI's work by hand.
